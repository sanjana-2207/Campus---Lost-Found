Project Title: Campus Lost and Found Management System

Description: A Java and MySQL-based application to manage lost and found items within a college campus, helping students and staff report, search for, and recover their belongings efficiently.

OOPS Concepts: Classes, Objects, Inheritance, Encapsulation, Abstraction, and Polymorphism.

DBMS Concepts: Tables, Primary Keys, Foreign Keys, CRUD Operations, SQL Joins, Constraints, and Transactions.

Technologies Used: Java, MySQL, JDBC, and Java Swing.

Main Features:

* Report lost and found items with descriptions, dates, and locations.

* Search and filter items by category and location.

* Identify potential matches between lost and found reports.

* Submit ownership claims and verify rightful owners.

* Track claim status and item recovery.

* Generate reports of lost, found, and recovered items.

Objective: To simplify the process of recovering lost belongings on campus through a centralized platform while demonstrating the practical application of OOPS and DBMS concepts.
